More Than Just a Flower Shop

With over 20 years of experience in the floral industry, our talented team has become a trusted business for beautiful weddings, special events, and everyday moments.

After purchasing our establishment 10 years ago, we extended our business to include a boutique, unique gifts, and a charming bakery and coffee shop—the Itty Bitty Bakery. We truly are a one-stop shop, offering multiple options for your big day and everyday life.

Our Services

Fresh Floral Design

As an Arkansas Master Florist (AMF) certified shop, we provide exquisite fresh flowers for all occasions. From everyday bouquets to elaborate wedding setups and custom sympathy sprays.

  • Custom Wedding Flowers & Decor
  • Sympathy & Funeral Arrangements
  • Daily Deliveries to Local Hospitals & Funeral Homes

Itty Bitty Bakery

Indulge your sweet tooth! We offer an array of freshly baked goods daily, plus custom orders for your special events.

  • Custom Cakes (Birthdays, Weddings, Showers)
  • Gourmet Cupcakes (Including Gluten-Free options!)
  • Cookies, Homemade Fudge, and Scones
  • Famous Wednesday Take-and-Bake Meals & Lunches

Coffee & Custom Drinks

Our menu is out of control—and that's the way we like it! If you don't see something you love, we'll gladly make a personalized drink with all your specifications.
