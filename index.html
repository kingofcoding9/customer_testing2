Our History

Amelia's Picalily Flowers and More is a local Nashville, Arkansas, florist offering fresh flowers and creative gift ideas for weddings, special events, celebrations, sympathy, and everyday moments.

Along with floral design, we offer gourmet and fruit baskets, baked goods, homemade fudge, plants, home décor, baby gifts, balloons, plush animals, and other unique gifts. The Itty Bitty Bakery operates as part of Amelia's Picalily and makes handmade treats with quality ingredients and scratch recipes.

Pricing & Substitution Policy

Prices and fees: Product prices do not include applicable service fees, delivery fees, taxes, or optional tips. These charges are calculated before payment during checkout and may vary by delivery location, timing, and handling requirements.

Substitutions: Seasonal availability may require substitutions. Picalily will preserve the arrangement's overall style, theme, and value whenever substitutions are necessary.

Returns and replacements: If there is a concern with an order, call Picalily directly to discuss the available return or replacement options.
